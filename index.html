September 2026

A web-based ERP system for industrial warehouse, inventory, and supply chain management, streamlining stock tracking and operations with a centralized, real-time dashboard.

Project Details:

StockSense ERP is an industrial warehouse management system designed to digitize and streamline inventory, stock movement, and supply chain operations. The platform provides centralized tracking of stock levels, warehouse locations, and inventory transactions, replacing manual, error-prone record-keeping with a real-time, data-driven system.
